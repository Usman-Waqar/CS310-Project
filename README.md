## App Overview

**SUSWallet** is a cross-platform mobile application built with Flutter that provides university students and staff with a single
digital platform to deposit funds, allocate them to their physical Student ID card (SUCard), and track card-tap payments across the
three main campus services – printing, shuttle transportation, and cafeteria dining.

Campus members previously relied on the third-party application SoliClub to deposit money into their SUCard and distribute it
across campus services. With SoliClub discontinuing its service, students no longer have access to a unified mobile platform to
manage their balance for daily campus transactions. Because campus printing, shuttle transportation, and cafeteria dining
operations are each managed by separate vendors with independent and unrelated financial accounts, funds allocated to one
service (e.g., 100 TL allocated to printing) cannot be spent or redistributed to another service (e.g., shuttles) and must be isolated in dedicated vendor sub-wallets prior to vendor settlement.

The app is intended for undergraduate and graduate students, faculty members, and administrative staff who use their SUCard to
pay for campus shuttles, printing stations, and cafeteria dining. In addition, it allows campus vendors to receive and settle service
payments.

Users log in with their university credentials, load funds into the unallocated Main Wallet, transfer specific amounts into
dedicated SUCard Service Sub-Wallets (Printing, Shuttle, and Cafeteria), manage their SUCard status (e.g., NFC card linking
and instant card freeze if lost), simulate or perform NFC card taps for testing, and track their real-time tap history.
